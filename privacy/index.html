Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

This policy is intended to describe Solis's current data practices. It is not legal advice.

1. Who We Are

Solis is operated by Solis Servives. For privacy questions, contact solis-auth@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Solis, we may process:

  • Account information such as your email address and authentication information.
  • App settings and preferences needed to provide and remember your configuration.
  • Technical information required for the application, authentication, security, troubleshooting, and service operation.

Solis does not currently use an analytics platform.

3. Authentication and Third-Party Services

Solis uses Supabase for account authentication and session management. You may also sign in through supported third-party identity providers such as Google or Discord. Those providers may process information under their own privacy policies.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to create and maintain your account, authenticate sign-ins, save app settings, operate and secure Solis, provide requested features, respond to support requests, and maintain the service.

5. Cookies and Browser Storage

Solis and connected authentication or web services may use cookies, local storage, session storage, and related browser technologies. These technologies can be used to keep sessions active, remember preferences, support authentication, and operate third-party websites opened through the application.

See the Cookie Policy for additional information.

6. Chess Websites and Other Third-Party Sites

Solis may open or interact with third-party websites such as chess platforms. Information processed on those websites is governed by the third party's own policies and terms. Solis does not control the privacy practices of third-party sites.

7. Sharing and Disclosure

Solis does not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed to service providers that help operate authentication, hosting, security, or other necessary infrastructure, or when disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

8. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No online or computer system can guarantee absolute security.

9. International Transfers

Some service providers may process information in countries other than the Philippines. Where applicable, transfers are handled subject to the provider's terms and applicable data-protection requirements.

10. Retention

We retain account information and settings for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain the account, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. You may request deletion of your account.

11. Account Deletion

You may request account deletion from within Solis where the feature is available, or by emailing solis-auth@gmail.com. We may retain limited information where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate obligations.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights regarding access to, correction of, deletion of, or other control over your personal information. Requests can be sent to solis-auth@gmail.com.

13. Children

Solis is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.
