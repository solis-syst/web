Cookie Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

This policy explains cookies and related browser storage used by Solis or services opened through it.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of data stored by a website or web service in a browser. Similar browser technologies include local storage and session storage.

2. How Solis Uses Them

Cookies or browser storage may be used to support authentication sessions, remember settings, maintain secure sign-in flows, and operate web content that is opened inside or alongside the application.

3. Authentication Providers

Supabase, Google, Discord, or other authentication services may set or read cookies or browser storage as part of their own authentication flows. Their use of those technologies is governed by their respective policies.

4. Third-Party Websites

Chess platforms and other websites accessed through Solis may use their own cookies and tracking technologies. Solis does not control those technologies or their purposes.

5. Analytics

Solis does not currently use an analytics platform.

6. Managing Cookies

Cookie controls are generally provided by the browser or web service that sets the cookie. Blocking or deleting cookies may prevent authentication or other website features from working correctly.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated when Solis's browser, authentication, or web integrations change.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to solis-auth@gmail.com.
